Partnership Response

We are in.
Let us supercharge every wallet.

Your brief is right: Tael answers how an agent gets paid, but not what happens when it cannot pay, or what it can do with what it earns. Fianza is both answers. Here is exactly what we will build, what we need from you, and the order we ship it in.

On Tael, AI agents pay for APIs and earn from their own. Fianza turns what they earn into credit they can borrow against. We are ready to make every payment we settle legible to you.

01 · The vision

What we are building toward

Every hot wallet becomes a supercharged wallet

Today, an agent whose balance runs low simply stalls, and we even hide agents that cannot pay. With a Fianza credit line on each Tael wallet, that never happens: the agent's cash covers what it can, and the credit line quietly covers the rest. Agents borrow on demand against the revenue they earn on Tael, repay it from ongoing income, and see their limit grow as they repay on time.

Credit becomes a capability any builder can switch on

Fianza will be a first class capability in the Tael marketplace. Any agent built on Tael that wants a credit score, a credit line, or revenue based financing simply calls the Fianza capability through our gateway, on the same pay per call USDC rails as everything else. Builders never integrate Fianza themselves. Tael builds it for them, and they turn it on.

Tael becomes the financial layer for agents

Payments, credit, and financing all run through one platform, on one chain, in one asset. A Tael operator with surplus USDC can even lend into Fianza vaults, earning yield on the very USDC we just settled for them. The money never leaves the Tael and Fianza loop.

02 · Division of work

Who builds what

The seam between us is clean. Here is the split.

Tael ships this
  • Payment attribution. A memo or tag on every settlement, so a transfer is traceable to Tael, and to a specific capability, from on chain data alone.
  • Richer receipts. Add amount and asset to SettlementReceipt, which today carries only txHash, payer, network, and settledAt.
  • Replay protection. A transaction hash uniqueness constraint, so no settlement is double counted.
  • Credit in the dashboard. Show each agent's credit line beside its USDC balance: limit, used, available, and a borrow action.
  • Fianza as a capability. List it in the marketplace so credit is one click to enable for any builder.
  • A testnet endpoint your demo agent can point at, so the proof loop runs with no work on your side.
Fianza brings this
  • A credit verifier. A Fianza backed PaymentVerifier that gates settlement on a credit line. Our verifier is a swappable interface (packages/payments/src/verify.ts), so this is a one line config swap, no fork.
  • The buyer client. Your payWithCredit(url, price) draw on 402 client, which is the piece Tael does not ship.
  • Revenue underwriting. Once we ship attribution, score a payTo address's Tael revenue and underwrite credit lines against it.
  • The demo agent, pointed at our testnet endpoint, to close the proof loop.
  • Co-documentation, so Fianza is the named credit layer in our docs and Tael is a named revenue source in yours.
  • Mainnet coordination when we move off test USDC to Circle's canonical asset.

There is no bridge to build. Both sides already speak the same primitive: x402 payments in Stellar USDC, on testnet today and mainnet next.

03 · The keystone

The one change that unlocks underwriting

Everything on the credit side depends on one fix, and it is entirely ours to make.

Why it matters

Your underwriter cannot yet tell Tael revenue apart, because our settlements are bare Operation.payment transfers with no marker, and the receipt omits the amount and asset. Adding a memo convention and those two receipt fields turns every Tael payment into a self attributing revenue signal you can read directly. That single change on our side is what makes a builder's Tael income score as creditworthiness.

04 · Rollout

The order we ship it in

Phase 0Both

Proof demo. A runnable end to end loop: a Tael wrapped API, a Fianza agent paying it on credit via payWithCredit, and one visible Stellar transfer with a matching Fianza ledger update. Uses our existing Stellar verifier as is.

Phase 1Fianza

Drop-in credit verifier. Fianza ships a PaymentVerifier that gates settlement on a credit line. A Tael operator adopts it with a one line config swap, alongside the mock and Stellar verifiers.

Phase 2Tael, then Fianza

Attribution and underwriting. Tael ships the memo convention and receipt fields. Fianza then indexes a payTo address's Tael revenue as a first class, self attributing source and underwrites against it.

Phase 3Both

Credit as a capability. Fianza is listed as a first class Tael capability, credit lines appear on every agent wallet, and both sides co-document the SDK surface.

Tael turns any API into a paid API. Fianza makes sure the agent can always afford it, and turns what it earns into what it can borrow. We record every payment; you read it as creditworthiness. Together, that is the whole loop on one chain.
