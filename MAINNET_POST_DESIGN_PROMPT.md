## Design direction
- Fianza brand: obsidian/near-black background, warm **amber/gold** (#FFB020)
  as the primary accent (NOT teal), mint/ion green (#58F0C8) secondary, bone
  off-white (#EDEDE7) text, subtle grain/noise texture.
- Serif display type for the headline (large, confident, 1-2 lines max),
  clean mono for the contract address / metadata (small, bottom corner —
  reads as "receipts," not decoration).
- A small "live" indicator: a pulsing dot + "MAINNET" badge, amber or ion.
- Optional subtle motif: three small connected nodes/blocks (representing the
  3 contracts) or a simple on-chain/ledger visual — nothing busy, this is a

  (no rockets, no moon, no candlestick charts).
- Must look good as a static screenshot (no dependency on interaction/hover).
- Include the Fianza wordmark (text "Fianza" in the sans/display font)
  top-left or top-center, small.

Keep it clean and uncluttered — one headline, one supporting line, one badge,
one link, one small address. Nothing else competes for attention.
